# PHASE 9B — CONCEPT C: MINIMAL LUXURY GALLERY ("THE CURATED SALON")

**Concept Title:** Concept C — Minimal Luxury Gallery  
**Visual & Philosophical Reference:** Swiss Minimalist Graphic Design, Gagosian Fine Art Portfolios, Peter Zumthor Architecture, Aesop Spatial Catalogues.  
**Primary Aesthetic:** Pure bone white (`#FFFFFF`), raw natural linen (`#F7F5F0`), hairline charcoal borders (`#E5E0D8`), stark jet black typography (`#0A0A0A`), and zero decorative gradients.

---

## 1. Compositional Philosophy & Atmosphere

Concept C strips away all visual clutter to present residences with the purity and precision of a **modern art gallery catalogue**. There are no heavy pill buttons, no floating gradients, no colorful badge chips, and no decorative iconography. 

Instead, architecture is celebrated through **rigorous Swiss grid alignment**, **flawless typographic scale**, and **uncompromising photographic proportions**. Every element serves to direct absolute focus onto the physical form, materials, and light of the residences.

## 3. Detailed Component Architecture

### 3.1 Master Registry Header
- Fixed height: 60px.
- Grid: Strict 3-part layout:
  - Left: `BILEKSA — ARCHITECTURAL ARCHIVE · EST. 2026` (Sans-serif, 11px uppercase, tracking 0.25em).
  - Center: Clean text links with numerical indexing (`01/ Residences`, `02/ Portfolios`, `03/ Vision`, `04/ Private Desk`).
  - Right: Minimalist action text link: `REQUEST PRIVATE VIEWING [→]`.

### 3.2 Property Index with Interactive Hover Previews (Section 3)
- An elegant, hyper-clean text table listing the 6 residences.
- When the user hovers over any property row:
  - The row subtly shifts to warm bone background.
  - A crisp, floating landscape preview thumbnail (IMG-06 to IMG-11) appears smoothly following the cursor or pinned in an adjacent gallery viewport.
  - Displays location, price, and carpet area in aligned monospace columns.

### 3.3 Museum-Style Exhibition Plates (Section 4)
- Photography is presented with generous white matting (resembling fine art gallery framing).
- Below each photograph is an architectural museum label plaque:
  - *Title:* Plate 04 — The Aurelia Estate
  - *Architectural Element:* Cantilevered Marine Limestone Loggia
  - *Location:* Cap d'Antibes, Côte d'Azur
  - *Dimensions:* 1,450 m² Internal / 2,800 m² Grounds

### 3.4 Floor Plan Archive (Section 5)
- Renders the authentic 2D and 3D architectural schematics (IMG-24, IMG-25) against a crisp white drawing board.
- Clean vector line drawings with interactive level switching (Subterranean, Ground, Upper Level, Roof Terrace).

## 4. Strengths & Target Audience

- **Emotional Resonance:** Understated, severe, highly intellectual, supremely confident.
- **Client Perception:** The architectural equivalent of a blue-chip art gallery or bespoke Swiss watchmaker.
- **Ideal For:** Sophisticated institutional investors, architectural purists, and ultra-high-net-worth connoisseurs.
